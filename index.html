What Soluon is about

Who Soluon is

Soluon is a South African infrastructure-technology company founded in 2024. We are engineers building the hardware and software that give households, clinics, schools and farms dependable power and water.

The problem we solve

Load shedding, unreliable water and patchy connectivity are everyday engineering conditions here. Most "smart" technology quietly assumes a stable grid, fast internet, a smartphone and the cloud. We don't.

Our broader vision

A distributed, sovereign and circular infrastructure layer made of resilient "nodes" that stay useful across changing technologies, ownership cycles and generations, scaling from one home to one community.

What we've seen

"I check my borehole by walking 2 km. I buy diesel because I don't know my solar status. I lose a crop because I didn't know the pump failed." That is the daily reality that led the team to build Soluon.

What makes us different

One platform, not four gadgets. It works offline, needs no smartphone, keeps your data on your own device, and is built to be repaired and reused rather than thrown away.

Built with communities

We reach people through trusted local networks (spaza shops, co-ops, churches and clinics) rather than building new ones. If a shop already sells airtime, it can sell a NODE.

One platform

From one home to one community.

Everything Soluon builds runs on NODE, a modular platform that scales from a single household to a clinic, a school or a whole village. The same hardware serves all three; only the firmware and peripherals change.

The problem: infrastructure that fails the people who need it most

What is broken?

Power cuts, failing water supply and limited connectivity are treated as exceptions. For millions of South Africans, from city apartments to deep rural villages, they are the normal operating conditions.

Households guess when a tank is low, buy diesel without knowing their solar status, and only discover a pump failure when the crop is already lost. Meanwhile each device, from each brand, works alone and usually needs a phone and the cloud.

How Soluon works

Calm, local and built to last.

  1. 01

    Local-first

    Processing, storage and automation happen on the NODE in your home or community. If the internet disappears, the system still works. The cloud is optional, never required.

  2. 02

    You own your data

    Household and community data stays local and sovereign. We don't monetise data, and there is no "phoning home".

  3. 03

    Community-owned

    Shared systems can be run by elected community trustees, supported by trained local dealers, with payment models that fit the way people earn, including seasonal payment holidays.

  4. 04

    Works within real constraints

    No smartphone needed: control and monitor by SMS, USSD or voice menu on any phone. Built to survive dust, heat and intermittent power.

What we build

NODE is the platform. These are the production-ready systems at its core, each one repairable, offline-capable and designed to disappear into the environment until needed.

Production-ready

NODE Core

The universal infrastructure computer every Soluon deployment has. Local processing, storage, automation, dashboard, voice menu and on-device intelligence.

Production-ready

NODE Power Plug

See what every appliance uses. Monitors voltage, current, power and energy; switches loads with a relay; and protects against overcurrent, overvoltage and overpower.

Production-ready

NODE Water Sense

Know your water before it's gone. Measures flow, detects leaks, controls valves and applies safety rules locally, even with no connection.

SoluonApp

Your NODE, on your screen. See energy and water at a glance and take control from a dashboard, and for everyone without a smartphone, the same information is available by SMS, USSD or voice.

Who we serve & who we work with

Government

Municipalities and departments procuring resilient infrastructure for rural development programmes, clinics and schools.

Communities

Households, villages, spaza shops and congregations, with shared systems run by trusted community trustees.

Organisations

Agricultural co-ops, NGOs, development-finance partners, faith-based organisations, clinics and schools.

Skilled workers

Rural dealers, field agents and technicians who install, repair and refurbish NODEs, and earn commission and refurbishment credit doing it.

For shop owners and local agents

Your shop can be the last mile.

If a spaza shop already sells airtime and batteries, it can sell and support NODE. Dealers handle first-line troubleshooting, basic repairs and onboarding, and earn commission on subscriptions plus credit for refurbished units they return.

No internet is needed to onboard a new customer. A dealer with a feature phone can activate a NODE through USSD.

Our principles

Community ownership

Data sovereignty
